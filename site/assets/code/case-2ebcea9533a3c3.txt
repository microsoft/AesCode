Myths and stigma surrounding bipolar disorder often lead to misunderstandings, making it harder for individuals to receive accurate diagnoses, appropriate treatment, and necessary support.

      <div class="panel fact">
        <h4>FACT</h4>
        <p>Children and adolescents can be affected by bipolar disorder, although symptoms may manifest differently than in adults. It is sometimes misdiagnosed as ADHD or behavioral issues. Specialized pediatric mental health evaluation is essential for proper diagnosis and treatment.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="row row3">
      <div class="divider-line"></div>
      <div class="panel myth">
        <h3>3. Bipolar disorder has no successful treatment.</h3>
      </div>
      <div class="icon-circle">
        <svg viewBox="0 0 48 48">
          <path d="M12 10h24v26H16l-4 4 4-6H12z"></path>

      <div class="panel fact">
        <h4>FACT</h4>
        <p>Bipolar disorder is a highly treatable condition. A combination of medication, such as mood stabilizers, antipsychotics, or antidepressants, and psychotherapy can be effective for many individuals. With proper ongoing care, people can manage symptoms and lead fulfilling lives.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="row row4">
      <div class="divider-line"></div>
      <div class="panel myth">
        <h3>4. Bipolar disorder is nothing more than an overactive imagination.</h3>
      </div>
      <div class="icon-circle">
        <svg viewBox="0 0 48 48">
          <path d="M24 10c-7 0-12 5-13 12-5 1-8 5-8 10 0 7 6 12 14 12h18c8 0 14-5 14-12 0-5-3-9-8-10-1-7-6-12-13-12z"></path>

      <div class="panel fact">
        <h4>FACT</h4>
        <p>Bipolar disorder is a serious, chronic brain condition, not an issue of personality or imagination. It involves biological differences and neurochemical imbalances that significantly impact mood, energy levels, and behavior, warranting medical attention and understanding.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="row row5">
      <div class="divider-line"></div>

      </div>

      <div class="panel fact">
        <h4>FACT</h4>
        <p>Bipolar disorder is characterized by episodic mood swings, with periods of stability between manic/hypomanic and depressive episodes. The duration, frequency, and intensity of these episodes vary significantly among individuals, and many experience symptom-free periods.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

        <p>Medication is often a long-term component of managing bipolar disorder. Stopping medication abruptly, even when feeling well, can lead to a relapse of symptoms and potentially severe complications. Adjustments should always be made under the guidance of a healthcare provider.</p>
      </div>
